# Basket: getting started

Basket is a shared shopping list. Anything one person adds shows up on the other phones straight away. Setup takes about 3 minutes.

## 1. Install it on your phone

Open the Basket link you were sent, then add it to your home screen so it opens like a normal app.

**iPhone (use Safari, not Chrome)**

1. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
2. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. From now on, open Basket from its green icon on your home screen. On iPhone, notifications only work from the icon.

**Android (use Chrome)**

1. Tap the **three dots** at the top right.
2. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**), then **Install**.
3. Open Basket from its icon on your home screen.

## 2. Sign in (once)

1. Type the **household password** (ask whoever set Basket up; it is never written down here) and tap **Unlock**.
2. Type **your name**, or tap it if it's already listed, then tap **Continue**. It's how the others see who added what.

The phone stays signed in after that.

## 3. Turn on notifications

These tell you when someone else adds things, for example "Sam added 3 items: Milk, Eggs, Bread". They arrive a few minutes after the other person stops adding, so you get one notification instead of one per item.

1. Open Basket from the home screen icon.
2. Tap **Settings**, then scroll to **Notifications**.
3. Tap **Turn on**, then **Allow** when your phone asks.

If it says "Add Basket to your Home Screen first", go back to step 1 and open it from the icon.

## 4. Everyday use

There are two lists. The **Catalogue** keeps everything you usually buy, for good. **Shopping** shows only what's needed right now.

**At home: something has run out**

1. Tap **Catalogue**.
2. Tap the **circle** next to the item. It turns orange, which means it's on the shopping list.
3. Not in the Catalogue yet? Type it in the search box and tap **+ Add "…"**. Pick a category and the shops that sell it (prices are optional), then tap **Add to catalogue and list**.

**In a shop**

1. Tap **Shopping**.
2. Tap the shop you're in at the top (e.g. **Aldi**) to see only what you can buy there. **By shop** groups the whole list by shop instead.
3. Tap each item as it goes in the basket. It moves to "Picked this trip". Tapped the wrong one? Tap it again, or tap **Undo**.
4. At the till, tap **Finish trip**. Picked items leave the shopping list but stay in the Catalogue for next time.

**Scanning a barcode**

In the Catalogue, tap **Scan** and point the camera at the barcode, about 15 cm away. A product already in the Catalogue goes straight on the list. A new one opens with its name and photo filled in where possible; pick the category and shops. If a crinkly pack won't scan, type the number under the barcode into the box below the camera.

**Editing an item**

Tap an item's name in the Catalogue to change its category, shops, prices, photo or barcode, or to delete it.

**Not sure what something does?**

Tap the **?** at the top right. Then tap anything to see what it's for; nothing changes while info mode is on. Tap **?** again to finish.

## 5. Updates and troubleshooting

Updates install themselves. When there's a new version, a bar saying **"Update ready. Tap to reload"** appears under the title. Tap it.

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "That password isn't right" | Check it with whoever set Basket up. It's case-sensitive. |
| Someone else's changes don't show | Close the app completely and reopen it. Check you have a signal. |
| Scan shows a black screen or "Camera blocked" | Allow camera access for Basket in your phone's settings. iPhone may ask again each time you open the app. |
| A barcode won't scan | Hold the pack flat in good light, about 15 cm away, or type the number into the box under the camera. |
| No notifications | Settings > Notifications should say "On". On iPhone, open Basket from the home screen icon, not Safari. |
| Wrong name at the top right | Settings > "I'm …" > **Switch**. |
